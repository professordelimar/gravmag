# References

**Author:** Nelson Ribeiro-Filho

This file contains core references relevant to gravimetry, magnetometry,
potential-field theory, modeling, processing and inversion.

## Potential-field theory and interpretation

Blakely, R. J.  
*Potential Theory in Gravity and Magnetic Applications.*  
Cambridge University Press.

Hinze, W. J., von Frese, R. R. B., and Saad, A. H.  
*Gravity and Magnetic Exploration: Principles, Practices, and Applications.*  
Cambridge University Press.

Telford, W. M., Geldart, L. P., and Sheriff, R. E.  
*Applied Geophysics.*  
Cambridge University Press.

Menke, W.

Aster, R. C., Borchers, B., and Thurber, C. H.  
*Parameter Estimation and Inverse Problems.*

Tarantola, A.

## Use of references

Whenever a code directly implements, reproduces or adapts a published
algorithm, transformation, filter, forward-model equation or inversion method,
the corresponding reference should be stated in the script, notebook or
documentation.

Additional papers and books should be added here as the repository grows.
